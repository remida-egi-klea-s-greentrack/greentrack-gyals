# Kitchen appliances: dishwasher, refrigerator and cooking

Applies to: the kitchen sub-meter (sub_metering_1: dishwasher, oven and microwave; hot plates are gas in this household) and the refrigerator on sub_metering_2.

Advice for the dishwasher:
- Run full loads. A dishwasher uses almost the same energy and water whether it is half empty or full.
- Choose the no-heat or air-dry option instead of heated drying.
- Scrape plates instead of pre-rinsing them under the tap; skip rinse-hold cycles.
- Use the eco programme if there is one; otherwise match the programme to how dirty the load is.

Advice for the refrigerator and freezer:
- Keep the fridge at about 2-3 °C (35-38 °F) and a separate freezer at about -18 °C (0 °F). Colder settings use more energy without keeping food better.
- Place it away from the oven and direct sun and leave space behind it for air to circulate.
- A refrigerator or freezer older than about 15 years is worth replacing with an efficient model.

Advice for cooking:
- Use the microwave or a small appliance for small portions instead of heating the whole oven.

Sources: U.S. Department of Energy, Energy Saver, "Kitchen Appliances" fact sheet (2021) and "Dishwashers".
